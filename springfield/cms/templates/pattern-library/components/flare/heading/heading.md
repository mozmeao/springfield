The `heading` component is Flare's headline: a heading with an optional superheading above and subheading below, grouped in an `hgroup` when either is set. It renders nothing when all three texts are empty.

The Heading block nests in nearly every Flare block and takes its level from where it sits on the page (h2 by default), so editors never pick a level or size. Templates choose the size with **heading_size**, and **link** turns the text into a link.
