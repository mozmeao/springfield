The `showcase` component highlights one feature or product with a large media surface, a heading, supporting copy and a caption, with up to two CTAs. **layout** sets how wide the media runs: default, expanded or full width.

It's the Showcase block on free-form, What's New, Smart Window, home, referral and share pages. On the referral and share pages its media can be a set of tabs instead of an image. Provide desktop and mobile images so the media holds up on small screens.
