The `pencil-banner` component is the thin ribbon above the navigation: a short title and description, linked when there's a URL. Italic text in the title becomes a yellow pill.

Editors create a Pencil Banner Snippet and attach it in the Pencil Banners panel of a home, article, article theme or free-form page; a Conditional Display block in its **Settings** field targets audiences. Use pencil banners sparingly, and make non-essential ones **dismissable** — the close button hides the banner until the next page load.
