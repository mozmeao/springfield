The `card-gallery` component is a fixed grid of a main card (purple) and a secondary card (dark purple), each with an icon and an image, plus a smaller callout card with neither, under a heading and above an optional CTA.

It's rendered by the Card Gallery block, which only sits at the top level of a page: free-form pages, the homepage, and the referral and share pages. The main and secondary cards each hold at most one button, and the card headings sit one level below the gallery's heading.
