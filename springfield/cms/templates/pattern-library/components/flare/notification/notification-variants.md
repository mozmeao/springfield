The `notification` component is an inline alert with an optional icon and headline, in purple, green, orange or red. It's the Notification block on free-form, What's New and Smart Window pages and the thanks page; the contact form, blog and download templates use it directly.

Match the color to severity: purple for general info, green for success, orange for warnings, red for errors. **closable** adds a close button that removes the notification until the page reloads; leave it off when the visitor needs to act on the message.
