The `sharing-links` component is a row of three buttons for sharing one URL: copy it, email it, or show it as a QR code in a dialog. The URL also shows as a link above the buttons unless **show_url** is off.

It's the Sharing Links block, in Sections, Featured Image Sections, Media + Content, Form + Content, Two Column Cards and Banners. The email subject and body are optional, and the URL always goes at the end of the body. The QR dialog's heading and text are optional too.
