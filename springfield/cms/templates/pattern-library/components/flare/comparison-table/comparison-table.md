The `comparison-table` component lays out plain-text data — plans, tiers, feature support — in a table, behind the Comparison Table block on free-form and article pages, in Sections and in Tabs. Cells can span up to three columns, and empty cells just fill the space.

**highlighted_column** (1–4) gives a column a background, and **mobile_behavior** picks horizontal scroll (default) or stacked rows on small screens, with each row's label on its own line above its values; stacked mode drops the highlight. For browser logos and Yes/No results, use the Browser Comparison Table instead.
