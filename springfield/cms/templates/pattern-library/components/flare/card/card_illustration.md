The `card` component is Flare's composable card, rendered by the Card block inside a Cards List. An illustration card is the default variant with a full-width image in the `media` slot, above a heading, text and buttons. The same slot takes a 48px icon or a larger pictogram instead.

Illustration cards are the workhorse for product and feature roundups where each item needs equal weight. Keep illustrations consistent in style and color across a set. The Cards List sets the container width and how many cards share a row.
