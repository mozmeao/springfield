The sliding carousel steps through 2–6 slides on its own: a list of headings on one side, the matching image or video on the other, with a progress bar on the active heading. Each slide stays up for the length of the longest video, or 24 seconds. Below 900px the headings become a swipeable carousel with dots.

It's the Sliding Carousel block, on free-form, What's New and Smart Window pages; there's no component of its own, so this sample copies the block's markup. Keep slides visually consistent and keep links out of slide text — the motion makes them hard to click.
