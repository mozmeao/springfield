The `banner` component is the full-width call-out section behind the Banner block: a heading, content (rich text, tags, buttons) and optional media — an image, video, animation or QR code. The Banner Snippet uses it too when its Kit theme is off, with a fixed bottom-cta look.

Use **theme** to set its visual weight: default, outlined, purple radial gradient, or dark purple gradient and its inverted version. The purple themes always render in dark mode. **media_after** moves the media after the text on desktop, and **slim** reduces the padding. Use banners sparingly — only when there's a CTA.
