The `tag` component is a small pill label with an optional icon before or after the text, in purple or grey. **is_selected** marks the active state used by filters, like the Roadmap's platform tags.

Editors add tags to Intro, Banner, Kit Banner, Media + Content, Card, Form + Content and Related Article blocks, up to three per tag list, picking a title, color, icon and icon position. Two Column Cards take a single text-only tag. The blog topic style isn't an editor choice; blog templates apply it.
