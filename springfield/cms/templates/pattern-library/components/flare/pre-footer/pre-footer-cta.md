The `pre-footer-cta` component is the final call to action above the footer: a single Download Firefox button. It's the Pre Footer Call to Action snippet, which only sets the button's label and analytics ID.

Free-form pages show it unless **Show Pre-Footer** is turned off in their settings; Share Firefox pages have to turn it on; home and article pages always show it. Only non-Firefox visitors see it; Firefox users get the newsletter sign-up instead. Enterprise-themed pages can't turn it on.
