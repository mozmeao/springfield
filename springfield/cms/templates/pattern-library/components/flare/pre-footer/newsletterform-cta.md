The `newsletter-form` component is the pre-footer newsletter sign-up: a heading, a subheading and the email form with country and language fields, beside a Kit or globe illustration, or none.

On pages that show the pre-footer, Firefox users see it instead of the download CTA. The Pre Footer Call To Action Form snippet sets its heading and subheading. Free-form and What's New pages pick the illustration in their **Pre-footer options** setting; other pages show Kit. The download, What's New and newsletter pages include it directly.
