The `stacked-article-list-item` component is one row of a stacked list of articles: a pictogram, a title, a short description and a small ghost button. Rows stack vertically, one per line.

It's the Article Cards List block with its card type set to **Pictogram Row**, in Sections and Featured Image Sections. Each row links to an article or article theme page and can override its title, description, pictogram, link label and link; without a pictogram it shows the Firefox logo.
