The `step-card` component numbers a card in a sequence — a how-to, a setup walkthrough — with cards running left to right. Each card has an image, an optional eyebrow, a headline, optional content and at most one link button.

It's the Step Cards List block, in Sections and Featured Image Sections. Limit it to 3–4 steps so the flow stays scannable, and keep each step action-oriented: a verb in the headline, one supporting sentence. **expand_link** makes the whole card clickable.
