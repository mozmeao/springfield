The `form-content` component places a Contact Page's form beside a content column — heading, tags, rich text, buttons and sharing links. It's the Form + Content block on free-form pages and in Sections; the form loads from the chosen contact page, so the block renders nothing when that form can't be shown.

The form follows the content by default. **form_before** puts it first on desktop; on mobile it always comes after the content. The form's **Two Column Layout** setting puts its fields in two columns on large screens.
