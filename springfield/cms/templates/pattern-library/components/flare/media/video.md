The `video` component shows a poster with a play button and only loads the player when clicked: a privacy-friendly YouTube embed, or a native player for videos hosted on assets.mozilla.net. Other sources are rejected by the block and show an error here.

Editors add it through a media field — in Intros, Media + Content and other blocks — or as a Video block on article pages, with a required poster, an **aspect_ratio**, and alt text that becomes the play button's label. Write the alt text to say what the video is about.
