The `enterprise-download` component is the Firefox Enterprise download section: a heading over three platform menus — Windows, macOS and Linux — each a `dropdown` with the latest release, its alternative installer (MSI, PKG or ARM64), ESR, and the ESR alternative. Links, labels and the download language are built in.

The Enterprise Download block, on free-form pages, sets the **section heading** plus an optional heading and rich text below the menus; leave both blank to drop that part. **center_content** centers that heading and text.
